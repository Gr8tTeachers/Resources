✅ Answer Key – Activity 1

Q1. Large purchase
→ large — A "large purchase" means buying something expensive, like a fridge or washing machine.
Q2. Finding the best price
→ cheapest — They compared shops to find the lowest price.
Q3. Reduction in price
→ discount — A discount means you pay less. E.g. 15% off.
Q4. Shop brings item to your home
→ delivery — You pay extra for delivery (e.g. $50–$90).
Q5. Promise to repair/replace
→ warranty — An extended warranty gives you extra protection.
Q6. A company that sells goods
→ retailer — Examples: Harvey Norman, JB Hi-Fi, The Good Guys.
Q7. Booklet listing prices
→ catalogue — Many shops have online or paper catalogues.
Q8. Extra items you can choose
→ add-ons — Add-ons cost extra on top of the main price.
Q9. Looking at different shops
→ compare prices — Comparing prices helps you save money.
Q10. Percentage is out of ___
→ 100 — Percent means "per hundred". 20% = 20/100 = 0.20.

✅ Answer Key – Activity 2

Q1. How many stores?
→ 3 stores (The Good Guys, Harvey Norman, JB Hi-Fi)
Q2. Biggest discount?
→ JB Hi-Fi with 20% off
Q3. Add-ons at The Good Guys?
→ Delivery ($80) and extended warranty ($120)
Q4. 10% of $1,280
→ $128.00 ($1,280 × 0.10 = $128)
Q5. Final price at The Good Guys
→ $1,152.00 ($1,280 − $128 = $1,152)
Q6. Final price at Harvey Norman (5% off $1,150)
→ $1,092.50 (5% of $1,150 = $57.50 → $1,150 − $57.50 = $1,092.50)
Q7. Final price at JB Hi-Fi (20% off $1,400)
→ $1,120.00 (20% of $1,400 = $280 → $1,400 − $280 = $1,120)
Q8. Total with add-ons
→ $1,352.00 ($1,152 + $80 + $120 = $1,352)
Q9. Cheapest store (after discount)
→ Harvey Norman at $1,092.50
