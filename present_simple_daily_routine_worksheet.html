Present Simple Tense: Daily Routine

Interactive worksheet: drag the words to complete the sentences and match daily routine activities.

Grammar Reminder

I / you / we / they

I get up at 7 o'clock. They go to work by bus.

he / she / it

She gets up at 7 o'clock. He goes to work by bus.

Negative

I don't drink coffee. He doesn't watch TV in the morning.

Questions

Do you have breakfast? Does she study English?
